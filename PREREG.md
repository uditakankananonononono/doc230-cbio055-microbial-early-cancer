# CBIO055 "Early Cancer Detection Using Microbial Information" - preregistration (before any model scored)
Replication-style audit on public data (Poore et al. 2020 TCGA microbial abundances, Voom-SNM normalised, 1,287 genera, 17,625 samples). No novelty claim.
Known caveats stated up front: TCGA tumor vs normal differ by tissue, center and sequencing strategy; Poore data is contested (human-read contamination critique, Gihawi et al. 2023); stage I/II is "early" in TCGA staging only; no healthy-donor plasma, so this is tissue-microbiome, not liquid-biopsy, detection.
Early = pathologic stage I, IA, IB, IS, II, IIA, IIB, IIC, "I or II NOS". Normal = Solid Tissue Normal (D10: Blood Derived Normal). Splits grouped by case_uuid. Models: L2 logistic regression (C=0.1) on genera; metadata-only baseline = logistic regression on one-hot disease_type + data_submitting_center + experimental_strategy. 5-fold grouped CV.
Gates: D2 microbial AUROC >= 0.80 AND >= metadata-only baseline + 0.05 (pan-cancer early tumor vs solid-tissue normal). D4 mean leave-one-cancer-type-out AUROC >= 0.70 (does signal transfer).
Directions: D1 data stats; D2 pan-cancer early tumor vs normal; D3 per-cancer-type within-type CV (types with >=15 normals and >=15 early tumors) incl. metadata baseline; D4 leave-one-cancer-type-out; D5 train on late stage (III/IV) tumor + half of normal cases, test on early tumor + other half of normal cases; D6 cancer-type multiclass among early tumors (macro-F1 vs metadata baseline of center+strategy only); D7 label-shuffle control; D8 metadata-only baseline result as reported in D2/D3; D9 feature stability (top-20 |coef| genera Jaccard across folds, features listed with no biological claims); D10 early tumor vs Blood Derived Normal (flagged: confounded by tissue/sample type).
Not run: contamination filtering against human reads (needs raw reads); stated.
